# SimCPR PRO — Community & Feedback

Welcome to the official community space for **SimCPR PRO**, the CPR training, exam and certification app for instructors and training centers. SimCPR PRO connects over Bluetooth to **SimCPR ChestPad**, **SimCPR WristBand**, **Prestan** and **Laerdal Little Anne QCPR** devices, gives live feedback on compression depth, rate and recoil, and issues **PDF CPR certificates** to students who pass.

This is where you **report bugs**, **request features**, and **share feedback** to help shape the future of the app.

## 💬 Share Feedback & Report Issues

We welcome contributions from all SimCPR PRO users. The quickest way to help:

| | |
|---|---|
| 🐛 **Found a bug?** | **[Open a Bug Report →](https://github.com/SimCPR/SimCPRPro-Community/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)** |
| 💡 **Have an idea?** | **[Request a Feature →](https://github.com/SimCPR/SimCPRPro-Community/issues/new?template=feature_request.md)** |
| 🔎 **Want to browse?** | **[See all Issues →](https://github.com/SimCPR/SimCPRPro-Community/issues)** |

Before posting, please **[search existing issues](https://github.com/SimCPR/SimCPRPro-Community/issues)** to avoid duplicates, and see **[Submission Tips](https://github.com/SimCPR/SimCPRPro-Community/wiki/Submission-Tips)** for what makes a great report.

**Please do not post** personal or student data (names, dates of birth, certificates), account credentials, or purchase receipts.

## What this repository is for

- ✅ Collecting feedback on functionality, usability, and features
- ✅ Reporting bugs on iPhone, iPad and Android
- ✅ Requesting new features, device support, languages, and certificate options
- ✅ Community collaboration among CPR instructors and training centers

This repository is **not** for general support, account approval, or billing questions, and does **not** contain SimCPR PRO source code. For products and support, visit **[simcpr.com](https://simcpr.com)**. Store purchases and refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play.
